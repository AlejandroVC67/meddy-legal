2. Description of Service

Meddy is a personal health management app for iOS that allows you to organize health information for yourself and your family. Features include:

  • Family member profiles with photos
  • Medical records with body part categorization, tags, photos, document attachments, and PDF export
  • Appointment tracking with optional sync to Apple Calendar and configurable reminders
  • Health metrics tracking (custom metric types and entries)
  • A Home Screen and Lock Screen widget displaying upcoming appointments
  • iCloud sync across your personal devices

3. Free Tier and Subscription

Meddy is free to download. Free users may manage one (1) family member and view the calendar and metrics list.

The following features require a Meddy Premium subscription:

  • Adding a second or additional family members
  • Creating, viewing, or editing medical records
  • Exporting records as PDF
  • Creating or editing appointments
  • Creating or editing health metric types and entries
  • Managing custom Body Parts and Tags

Subscriptions are available on a monthly or yearly basis and are managed through Apple's App Store. Subscription management, billing, and entitlement validation are handled by RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Prices are displayed in your local currency at time of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.
